Smooth

Trace a photo into pieces

Draw around each part of the picture. The outline smooths when you let go, and you can drag the points if it needs a correction. Saving writes full-frame transparent PNGs, the same files prep_reveal already reads.

Scroll zooms toward the cursor. Hold Space and drag to pan. Right-drag cuts a piece back.
